How it works

Each organization is its own private workspace. Nothing you list or request is visible outside it.

  1. 1

    List what your unit doesn't need

    Add surplus supplies with a photo, the quantity, the condition, where they are, and when they can be collected. An admin reviews each listing before it goes live.

  2. 2

    Request what you do

    Say what your unit needs, how many, and by when. Choose pickup or delivery, and mark whether a similar item or a partial amount would work.

  3. 3

    Review a matched plan

    LoopLocal proposes a plan, possibly combining several units, and explains each choice. Submit it (admins approve it when your organization requires that), then arrange the hand-off.

A matching engine you can explain

The Resource Rescue Engine follows written rules. It is not artificial intelligence: the same request and the same inventory always produce the same plan.

  1. Filter first. Only approved items with stock left, from another unit in your organization, available by your date, the right kind of item, and a hand-off time that works for both sides are considered.
  2. Rank the rest. Exact matches come before substitutes, then better condition, then fewer separate hand-offs.
  3. Combine. If one unit can't cover the request, the plan draws from several.
  4. Explain. Every line says why that resource was chosen.

Two requests can never reserve the same stock. The server checks quantities inside a database transaction before anything is reserved.
